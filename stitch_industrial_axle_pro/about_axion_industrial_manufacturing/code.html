Axion Industrial is a tier-one manufacturer of critical mechanical components for the heavy trucking, construction, and agricultural sectors.

OUR LEGACY

Four Decades of Engineering Excellence

Founded in the industrial heartland, Axion Industrial began with a single objective: to produce components that withstand the most punishing environments on Earth. Today, we manage over 1.2 million square feet of manufacturing space across three continents.

Our commitment to vertical integration allows us to control every step of the process—from metallurgical testing to final precision machining. This ensures that every part stamped with the Axion mark meets our rigorous quality standards.
