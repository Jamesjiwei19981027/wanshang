How to Measure a Trailer Axle: Hub Face and Spring Center Guide

Precise measurements are critical for maintaining the structural integrity and tracking of industrial trailers. This guide outlines the standard engineering procedure for calculating Hub Face and Spring Center dimensions.

Critical Technical Diagrams

Figure 1.0: Axle Dimension Reference Matrix

1. Measuring the Hub Face (HF)

The Hub Face measurement is the distance between the two points on the axle where the wheel rims actually bolt onto. It is the most critical dimension for ensuring proper wheel clearance from the trailer frame.

rule Step-by-Step Procedure:

  1. Remove the wheels from the axle to expose the wheel studs.
  2. Hook your measuring tape on the outside of one hub face (the flat surface where the wheel meets the hub).
  3. Extend the tape across the axle to the exact same point on the opposite hub.
  4. Record the measurement in inches (standard industrial tolerance: ± 1/16").

2. Measuring the Spring Center (SC)

The Spring Center dimension determines where the leaf springs or torsion mounting brackets are welded to the axle tube. Incorrect SC measurement will result in mounting failure or severe frame stress.
