Marine Application Series

Superior Corrosion Resistance for Marine Environments

Engineering heavy-duty boat trailer components designed for continuous saltwater immersion. Featuring hot-dip galvanized finishes and pressurized bearing protection.

Marine Performance Engineering

Precision components built to withstand the rigors of marine transport and frequent saltwater exposure.

Hot-Dip Galvanized Coating

Our axles undergo a multi-stage hot-dip galvanization process, resulting in a 3.5mm thick zinc barrier that metallurgically bonds with the steel, providing cathodic protection that self-heals minor scratches.

ASTM A123 720HR Salt Spray Tested

Pressurized Bearing Protection

Maintain positive pressure inside the hub to prevent water ingress during hot-to-cold immersion. Features stainless steel springs and integrated grease nipples for easy maintenance.
